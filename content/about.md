# The Mentor Gap

![The Mentor Gap Logo](/img/the-mentor-gap-logo.png)

A mentor changes the shape of someone's career. Most people who reach senior positions in tech can point to someone who showed them the way. Most people who never get the chance simply never had that someone.
The Mentor Gap exists to close that distance, from both directions.

For mentees: free, self-serve guides that help you build your own AI mentor for job search support, study planning, and career growth when there is no human mentor within reach. The guides are built for real conditions: readable offline once downloaded, usable on a basic smartphone, and honest about what AI can and cannot do. They teach transferable skills, not shortcuts, and they will never pretend that an AI replaces a human mentor. They are built for people who currently have no mentor at all.

For new mentors: the Mentor Starter Kit gives people who want to mentor but don't know where to start the structure, questions, and tools a first mentorship requires. Confidence and structure are skills like any other, and they can be taught.

Everything is free to download.

The Mentor Gap is an independent, noncommercial initiative created by Suzana Melo, a full-stack engineer who transitioned into tech in her 40s with no prior technical background, rebuilt her career from scratch, and has spent years mentoring career changers, women, and tech beginners across Europe, West Asia, Africa, Asia-Pacific, and the Americas. Formal registration as a nonprofit association is planned as the project grows.

Growth starts where access does.
